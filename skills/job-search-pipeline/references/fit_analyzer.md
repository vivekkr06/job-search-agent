# Stage 2: Fit Analyzer

**Job:** evaluate how well the candidate matches one posting. Analyze and score only. Do not rewrite the resume or write cover letter content.

1. **Extract JD requirements first:** required skills, preferred skills, domain knowledge, seniority signals, regulatory or compliance requirements, red flags. Extract before scoring so scores are grounded in the posting. Mark every requirement the posting itself calls essential, required, a must-have, or says "matters most", and quote its wording; these are the essential requirements.
2. **Score five dimensions, 0 to 100, one sentence of rationale each:** Skill Match, Domain Alignment, Seniority Fit, Technical Depth, Regulatory / Compliance Fit.
3. **Overall score, weighted:** Skill Match 30%, Domain Alignment 25%, Seniority Fit 20%, Technical Depth 15%, Regulatory / Compliance 10%. Show the calculation.
4. **Gap analysis in two tiers.** Tier 1 Blockers: likely to eliminate the candidate from the shortlist. Any essential requirement (see step 1) that base_resume.md does not cover is automatically a Tier 1 blocker, even if the overall score is high; quote the posting's wording and say what evidence is missing. Tier 2 Risks: interview prep items. For each gap say whether it is closable before applying and how, and give an interview prep angle that reframes existing experience honestly.
5. **Strengths to Amplify:** 3 to 5 specific strengths from base_resume.md that match this role. This list is the handoff to the Resume Tailor.
6. **Verdict:**
   - APPLY NOW: overall score 75 or above and no Tier 1 blockers.
   - APPLY WITH PREP: overall score 55 or above, not meeting APPLY NOW, with at most one Tier 1 blocker and that blocker is closable before applying.
   - MONITOR: overall score below 55; or two or more Tier 1 blockers; or any Tier 1 blocker that cannot reasonably be closed before applying (for example, multiple years of experience in a core discipline).

Constraints: be honest, not generous; use only evidence in base_resume.md; never invent experience; state assumptions explicitly if the posting is vague; every item in KNOWN GAPS must be reflected in the gap analysis. Absence from base_resume.md means undocumented, not necessarily absent; word gaps as "not in base_resume.md" rather than "the candidate has never done X".

Save to `fit.md` with sections: JD Requirements Extracted, Fit Scores (table plus overall), Gap Analysis, Strengths to Amplify, Application Recommendation.
