# Stage 3: Resume Tailor

**Job:** rewrite the resume for one posting. Reframe, reorder, and reword existing experience. Never invent, fabricate, or exaggerate. Output must be ATS-compatible and in the candidate's own voice.

1. **Extract JD vocabulary:** 8 to 12 repeated or emphasized keywords, the seniority framing, and the top 3 outcomes the role is hired to deliver. Every bullet should connect to at least one.
2. **Mark each section** AMPLIFY, KEEP, DEPRIORITIZE, or OMIT. Never omit anything without recording it in the change log.
3. **Summary:** 3 to 4 sentences; open with the strongest match signal; use at least 3 JD keywords; do not name the target company.
4. **Work experience:** most relevant bullets first; reword with JD vocabulary only where honest; keep every metric exactly as written in base_resume.md (never round up or drift); add context only if it exists in base_resume.md; at most 6 bullets per role, and 2 to 3 for deprioritized roles. Do not rewrite older, less relevant roles heavily; they are credibility anchors.
5. **Skills:** reorder categories to lead with what this posting needs; add no skills, tools, or certifications that are not in base_resume.md.
6. **Change log** appended after the resume: what changed in the summary, each role, skills, omissions, and any gap from fit.md that is not in base_resume.md (flag it as an interview prep item, never fill it).

Constraints: every bullet must be traceable to base_resume.md; never name the target company inside the resume body; the candidate must be able to defend every line in an interview.

Save to `resume.md` (heading, title line, contact line, Summary, Work Experience, Education, Skills, Projects only if relevant, then CHANGE LOG).
