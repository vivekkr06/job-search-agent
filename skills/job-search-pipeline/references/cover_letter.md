# Stage 4: Cover Letter

**Job:** write a focused, specific, honest letter for one application, in the candidate's voice. Do not summarise the resume. Do not talk about what the candidate wants; talk about what the company gets.

1. **Think before writing:** the single biggest problem this hire solves; the one piece of evidence in base_resume.md that best proves the candidate can solve it; the most likely doubt a hiring manager has after reading the resume.
2. **Four paragraphs, 250 to 320 words total in the body:**
   - P1 (3 to 4 sentences): why this role at this company, naming something specific about their situation and connecting it to what the candidate does now. Never open with "I am writing to apply" or with a compliment.
   - P2 (4 to 5 sentences): the strongest proof point from Strengths to Amplify as situation, action, measurable result, using the posting's vocabulary.
   - P3 (3 to 4 sentences): a second proof point, or a Tier 2 gap handled directly and confidently as an adjacent strength with a bridge. Do not apologise.
   - P4 (2 to 3 sentences): state that the candidate wants the role and why now, plus one sentence on the first 90 days. End with conviction, not gratitude.
3. **Tone check:** no more than two sentences starting with "I"; avoid "passionate about", "excited to", "unique opportunity", "dynamic", "synergy", "leverage"; no sentence over 25 words; it must not read like a press release.
4. **Pre-writing note** after the letter: answers to the three questions, the strength chosen for P2 and why, the gap or second strength chosen for P3 and why.

Constraints: four paragraphs only; never mention what the candidate hopes to gain or learn; never fabricate company context (use only the posting or verifiable public knowledge); every claim must be supportable by base_resume.md; do not name a hiring manager unless the user provides one; the letter must add information beyond the resume. If the user asks for a different structure (for example an extra paragraph), follow the request and note it in the summary. If the fit verdict was MONITOR, confirm before writing.

Save to `coverletter.md` with a header (title, date, word count), candidate contact block, the letter body, and the pre-writing note.
